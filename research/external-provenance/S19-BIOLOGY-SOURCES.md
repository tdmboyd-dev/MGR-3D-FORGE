## Crucial qualifications

1. Plant grammar examples explain **possible morphogenesis**, not proof that an invented species grows or survives under the simulated environment.
2. Leaf count/angle/size metrics are measurable; beauty, photorealism and ecological viability are distinct and require their own evidence.
3. Biomechanical allometry differs by tissue, life stage, species and mode of locomotion; simple arbitrary scale multiplication is not scientific validation.
4. Ecosystem stability and resource accounting require independent dynamical checks over many time steps. A convincing screenshot says nothing about long-term viability.
5. Publicly accessible scientific PDFs and biodiversity records do not imply images, scan models, photographs, code or biological occurrence information can be redistributed commercially without review.
