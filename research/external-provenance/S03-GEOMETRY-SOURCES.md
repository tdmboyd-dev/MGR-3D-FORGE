## Risks not to erase

1. **The slicer cannot infer absent back-surface geometry from a single photo.** It consumes an authored/proposed mesh.
2. Geometric quality is multi-axis: visual smoothness, topology, solidness, physical thickness, deformation quality and printability are independent.
3. Naive exact-float vertex matching fails on near-coincident CAD intersections; tolerance, units and robust predicates are crucial. Tolerance-based welding itself can destroy small legitimate features.
4. STL has no standard multi-part object/semantic hierarchy; a full MGR wardrobe/character asset should retain a richer canonical scene/part contract and treat STL as a lossy delivery option.
5. GPU-free CPU slicing is plausible at bounded mesh sizes; high-detail slicing/remeshing runtime and memory must be benchmarked, never assumed.
6. **No installed third-party engines or model datasets were executed during this research packet.** Integration and licensing acceptance remain open.
