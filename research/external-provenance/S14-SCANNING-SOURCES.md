## Direct research implications

- A depth image is a grid of sensor observations with uncertainty; it is not a finished mesh or a guarantee of the unseen rear surface.
- ICP is local optimization. Symmetric shapes, low overlap, bad initialization and changing scenes can deliver plausible-looking but wrong alignments; a single residual threshold is insufficient.
- A low-noise TSDF result can still smooth away narrow features and can reflect pose drift. Keep source samples and measured/inferred labels.
- A LiDAR-phone `depthMap` is not universally survey-grade. True scale claims require calibration and independent measurement.
- Aerial geospatial cloud units/CRS/vertical datum and local-room camera coordinates are different contracts; explicit transforms and offsets are mandatory.
