## Audit status and rights

- **Verified so far:** real authoritative source locations, technical scope and product opportunities. **Not verified:** reproduction of every equation, quantitative benchmarks on our Windows device, full Pixar/Disney proprietary pipeline internals, every SIGGRAPH/GDC talk, and exact commercial rights for third-party data/assets.
- Production papers are teaching materials, **not a general license to reproduce copyrighted code, character designs, images, source footage or proprietary studio assets**. Algorithmic ideas inform independent MGR code; actual code/asset reuse requires a pinned source, exact licenses, notices, rights and tests.
- Industry material is used to find engineering **principles** and quality tests. MGR code, feature naming, identity, design requirements and acceptance remain native.
- Deeper targeted reading priority: measured BSDF equation/render parity; subdivision edge cases; skinned hair/cloth continuity; scene layers; many-light variance; subsurface scattering; streamed cell/actor safety; high-detail mesh seams; neural local edit confidence.
